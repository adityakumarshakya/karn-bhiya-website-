About Live4You Communication

An events services company in Delhi, New Delhi, focused on live communication.

Company overview

Live4You Communication (L4U) is in the business of physical, hybrid and virtual event management services, as well as webcast and video conferencing platforms.

Services range from live webcasts and video conferencing events to event production, live doctor symposiums, international speaker programs, live surgery broadcasts from the operation theatre, townhall webcasts, live AGM meetings, IR webcasts and live product launches.

The company’s public profile describes it as a live streaming and live broadcasting engineering business working across virtual and hybrid events and video conferencing on Zoom, Webex and Microsoft Teams. Services are available remotely, and pricing is on request.

Location: Delhi, New Delhi.

Live Streaming

Live broadcasts, including live surgery broadcasts from the operation theatre, produced for professional audiences.
